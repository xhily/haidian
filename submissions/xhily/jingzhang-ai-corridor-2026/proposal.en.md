# Jing-Zhang AI Path · Centennial Jing-Zhang Global AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

## Design Basis and Document Inventory

This proposal is based on the *Pre-Qualification Announcement for the International Urban Design Solicitation of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt* issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources[source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509], the excerpt from the taskbook *Open-Source Solicitation Taskbook for Global Agents on the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt*[source:SRC-2026-0518-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], and the provisional boundaries curated by the repository maintainers[source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]. At the normative level it cites the *Measures for the Administration of Urban Design*[standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], the *Measures for the Compilation and Approval of Regulatory Detailed Planning of Cities and Towns*[standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], and the *Guide for Land and Sea Use Classification for Territorial Spatial Survey, Planning and Use Control*[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

The official solicitation defines three tiers of scope: a coordinating study area of about 43.6 km², an overall design area of about 11.4 km², and three key areas totaling about 3.68 km²[metric:site_area_sqm][metric:key_areas_total_sqm]. Because the official precise boundary files are not yet ingested, this package uses the repository-provided provisional boundaries for generation, self-check, and display, and explicitly marks them as "provisional substitutes, not official redlines" in all related drawings and the main text[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. Once official polygons and regulatory conditions are completed, land use, metrics, and drawings must be recomputed.

## Three-Tier Scope Working Framework

The proposal establishes a progressive framework of "strategic research — overall design — key-area deepening" across the three spatial tiers[depth:three_level_scope_framework].

- **Coordinating study area (about 43.6 km²):** bounded by the North 5th Ring Road to the north, the Jingzang Expressway to the east, Xizhimenwai Street to the south, and the Wanquanhe Road to the west. It identifies the industrial gradient, talent flow, and regional coordination between northern Haidian — Zhongguancun — Xizhimen. It answers how the "belt" connects north to Future Science City and Huairou Science City, south to Financial Street and Lize Financial Business District, and west to the Three Hills and Five Gardens cultural belt.
- **Overall design area (about 11.4 km²):** centered on the urban districts and industrial zones within 1–2 km around the Jing-Zhang heritage park[metric:site_area_sqm]. The design depth reaches urban design at the regulatory-detailed-planning stage, coordinating industrial function, public space, slow mobility, urban renewal, and townscape control.
- **Key-area scope (about 3.68 km²):** from north to south, the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomy Acceleration Area (about 192.1 ha), the Beijing AI Origin Community (about 104.3 ha), and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (about 72.0 ha)[metric:key_areas_total_sqm]. The design depth reaches that of a comprehensive implementation plan, proposing an integrated "positioning — space — industry — scenario — operation" strategy.

All three tiers use `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` as spatial anchors, exchanging in `EPSG:4326` and recomputing areas in `EPSG:4548`[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001].

## Coordinating Study Area — Industry and Future-City Research

### Belt Master Concept and Functional Coordination

The proposal puts forward the master brand **"Jing-Zhang AI Path"**. The "Belt" in the English name echoes the linear heritage of the Jing-Zhang railway, while the Chinese "智径" (wisdom path) implies the pathway through which AI talent and scenarios converge[agent.1]. The logo direction uses the motif of "old railway sleepers + chip nodes + city skyline": two parallel abstract tracks symbolize the historical continuity of the Centennial Jing-Zhang, and three "wisdom points" embedded on the tracks correspond to the three key areas, together forming a visual symbol of the translation from "industrial heritage" to "intelligent infrastructure".

Three positions[source:SRC-2026-0518-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]:
1. **Centennial Jing-Zhang cultural belt:** using the Jing-Zhang railway heritage park as the backbone, showcasing the century-long narrative of China's indigenous innovation;
2. **Urban AI living-experience belt:** providing experienceable and participatable AI urban scenarios for talent, residents, and visitors;
3. **AI convergence innovation belt:** linking basic research, technology transfer, industrial application, and urban governance into an innovation corridor from "0 to 1, 1 to N".

Five functions[source:SRC-2026-0518-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]: a full-stack AI indigenous innovation system, a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, a new paradigm of AI+ scenario empowerment, an intelligent AI-vibrant city, and global discourse power in AI governance. They coordinate through "three zones, two wings": the three key areas are the innovation hard cores; the Zhongguancun technology-service wing is a research—incubation—financing—compliance service belt; the Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing is a public-experience and technology-validation belt.

### Global Case References

The proposal selects six groups of global experience as references for spatial and mechanism design (see table below); all cases come from public sources and are not directly copied. Key lessons: transforming linear infrastructure heritage into continuous public space (New York High Line), forming talent回流 through university–district coupling (Cambridge Kendall Square), lowering the threshold for AI urban application through "testbed — regulatory sandbox — scenario opening" (Singapore Jurong Lake District, Toronto Waterfront), building brand assets through annual conferences and developer communities (Barcelona MWC / SCEWC, Shenzhen Hi-Tech Fair), and unifying multi-source urban data through a digital-twin platform (Helsinki 3D City Model)[source:SOURCES-GLOBAL-CASES-2026].

## Overall Design Area — Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Detailed Urban Design

### Spatial Structure: "One Belt, Three Cores, Two-Wing Drive"

The overall design area takes the north–south Jing-Zhang heritage park vibrant belt as the "one belt", linking the northern Zhongzhiyuan core, the central AI Origin Community, and the southern Dazhongsi core; east–west it forms the "technology-service wing" along Zhongguancun Street–Xueyuan Road, and the "scenario-empowerment wing" along the Xiaoyue River–Xitucheng Road living corridor[data:geometry/land_use.geojson]. The land-use structure shows: AI industry and HQ business concentrate in the three cores; park green space and public space distribute continuously along the vibrant belt; talent housing and community services fill the two wings; the road micro-circulation and slow networks stitch the east–west barriers[metric:green_ratio][metric:public_space_ratio].

### Renewal Project List and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Due to the lack of existing-building inventory and ownership data, this proposal does not give a specific demolition, renovation, or new-construction list, but proposes directional classification by spatial location[depth:retain_renovate_demolish]:
- **Retain and upgrade:** Jing-Zhang railway historical structures, mature research institutes and university buildings along the line, and residential quarters with townscape value;
- **Functional replacement:** low-efficiency commerce, warehouses, and low-end offices along the line are vacated for AI showcases, co-working, and talent apartments;
- **New supplementation:** within the Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi key areas, supplement intelligent industry buildings, public labs, and community service facilities following the "small blocks, dense road network" principle.

Floor-area ratio, building height, and retain-renovate-demolish ratio are all set to `status=unknown`, to be recomputed after regulatory and existing-condition surveys are completed[metric:floor_area_ratio][metric:retain_renovate_demolish_ratio].

### Transportation, Rail, Municipal, and Public-Service Facilities

The proposal puts forward a transportation strategy of "rail-station integration + micro-circulation road network + continuous slow mobility"[depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. The Jing-Zhang vibrant belt main corridor is a slow-priority spine; east–west branch roads are densified to mitigate the cutting of space by current arterial roads; the key areas internally adopt 100–150 m small-block grids, reserving low-speed autonomous-driving shuttle and unmanned-delivery test paths[agent.3]. Existing rail transit (Line 13, Changping Line, Line 12, etc.) stations are connected to innovation nodes through underground corridors and second-floor bridges. For municipal and new infrastructure, the proposal conceptually lays out distributed edge-computing nodes, smart light poles, open sensing networks, and sponge-city facilities; specific routing and capacity require specialized municipal planning[depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Key-Area Detailed Design

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomy Acceleration Area (KEY-001, about 192.1 ha)

Positioning: AI full-stack indigenous innovation and hard-tech incubation core[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001].
Spatial strategy: organize space along the vertical innovation chain of "chip — algorithm — system — scenario"; national labs and university joint research institutes in the north, AI chip and computing mid-test platforms in the center, open testbeds and achievement-showcase plazas in the south. Land use is mainly industrial/R&D (1001) and business-finance (0902), embedded with park green belts and talent apartments.

### Beijing AI Origin Community (KEY-002, about 104.3 ha)

Positioning: world-class AI innovation ecosystem and talent community[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002].
Spatial strategy: around the Wudaokou–Tsinghua East Road West entrance innovation node, form a mixed "R&D — incubation — living service — public interaction" community. Land use is mainly research-education (0804), talent housing (0701), community service facilities (0702), and AI HQ business (0902). Focus on building a 24-hour innovation living room, shared experiment workshops, youth talent apartments, and an internationalized community center.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (KEY-003, about 72.0 ha)

Positioning: intelligent-native consumption and business-scenario cluster[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003].
Spatial strategy: with Dazhongsi Station as the TOD anchor, the ground floor lays out smart retail, AI experience stores, unmanned-delivery terminals, and flexible offices; upper floors are AI enterprise HQs and co-working. Land use is mainly business-finance (0902) and commerce (0901), combined with plazas and roof gardens to form three-dimensional public space.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Talent Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

### Talent and Enterprise Personas

The proposal proposes six core user personas:
1. **Hard-core researcher:** AI algorithm/chip scientists needing open compute, experiment platforms, and a quiet work environment;
2. **Young entrepreneur:** startup teams needing low-cost co-working, mentorship, and investment matchmaking;
3. **University teachers and students:** Tsinghua, Peking, Beihang and other university members needing industry–academia–research translation interfaces;
4. **Urban service provider:** community residents and operations staff needing accessible, easy-to-use public services;
5. **International visitor:** conference attendees and investors needing multilingual guidance and scenario experiences;
6. **Elderly and vulnerable groups:** needing retained offline services, human review, and age-friendly interfaces[standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW].

### AI Pilgrimage Landmarks

The proposal sets three AI pilgrimage landmarks[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]:
1. **Gate of Co-Creation:** south entrance of Zhongzhiyuan, with programmable light and open-source code projection as its skin, symbolizing the open entrance to AI indigenous innovation;
2. **AI Origin Beacon:** core node of the AI Origin Community, integrating a bell-tower imagery with real-time data art installations, displaying the global AI innovation index;
3. **Dazhongsi Compute Plaza:** plaza in front of Dazhongsi Station, with immersive light and interactive installations showing the urban-agent running status, hosting the opening of the annual developer conference.

Landmark design emphasizes rights clearance: no unauthorized fonts, trademarks, portraits, or corporate logos are used[agent.4].

## Transportation, Rail, Municipal, and Public-Service Facilities

The transportation system takes the Jing-Zhang vibrant-belt spine as the slow-mobility main corridor, densifying east–west branch roads to break the spatial barriers caused by the railway and expressways. The road-centerline `geometry/roads.geojson` has 28 lines totaling about 61.9 km, including urban secondary arteries and micro-circulation branches[metric:road_length_km]. Rail stations are integrated with key-area core nodes through underground corridors and second-floor bridges. The municipal system reserves conceptual routing for distributed edge-computing rooms, smart light poles, sensing equipment, and sponge-city facilities; specific pipelines and capacity require specialized municipal planning[depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Townscape

The blue-green space takes the Jing-Zhang heritage park as the north–south main green vein, linking the Qinghe–Xiaoyue River ecological corridor to form continuous park green space (1401) and protective green space (1402)[depth:blue_green_public_space]. The public-space system has three types: linear public space inside the heritage park, key-area gateway plazas (1403), and community pocket parks and street nodes. `geometry/green_space.geojson` and `geometry/public_space.geojson` are derived from `land_use.geojson`; the green ratio is about 16.7% and the public-space ratio about 17.7%[metric:green_ratio][metric:public_space_ratio].

Urban townscape control proposes the tone of "tech grey + eco green + Jing-Zhang rust": new buildings are mainly light grey and silver-white, echoing AI and chip imagery; Jing-Zhang railway historical structures retain rust-red and concrete texture; public space softens the hard-tech atmosphere with continuous greenery. The skyline follows "three cores slightly higher, two wings gradually lower, green corridor open", but specific height control awaits official regulatory clarity[metric:building_height_m].

## Renewal Project List, Implementation Policy, and Phasing Plan

### Renewal Project Directions (Conceptual)

1. **Jing-Zhang heritage park vibrant-belt upgrade:** linear-heritage display, slow system, smart guide, and public art;
2. **Zhongzhiyuan autonomy acceleration area:** labs, mid-test platforms, open testbeds, and talent apartments;
3. **AI Origin Community renewal:** old-building functional replacement into co-working, education workshops, and community services;
4. **Dazhongsi TOD smart-business core:** station-city integration, smart commerce, and public space;
5. **Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing:** low-speed autonomous-driving tests, AI+ public-service scenario demonstration belt;
6. **Zhongguancun technology-service wing:** legal, compliance, investment, and IP service nodes.

### Phasing Plan

`geometry/phasing.geojson` divides implementation into three phases[depth:phasing_implementation]:
- **Near term (2026–2028):** launch the Jing-Zhang vibrant-belt demonstration segment, the first batch of Zhongzhiyuan mid-test platforms, the AI Origin Beacon, and the Dazhongsi Compute Plaza node, forming visible and experienceable landmark segments.
- **Mid term (2028–2030):** expand AI Origin Community renewal, the Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing, and the smart-transport micro-circulation network; establish the developer community and annual-event brand.
- **Long term (2030–2035):** operate the complete system, drive full coordination of the three zones and two wings, and form a "pilgrimage land" that global AI innovation talent aspire to.

## Long-Term Operations Design

The proposal puts forward the "Jing-Zhang AI Path Annual Summit" as the core brand event, with fixed annual themes including: AI urban-governance forum, open-source model releases, developer hackathons, youth talent job fairs, and public open days[agent.6]. The operating mechanism includes: a DAO-style proposal process for the developer community, an apply—evaluate—feedback closed loop for scenario open-testing, a data dashboard linked with the urban-agent platform, and linkage communication with international conferences (such as WAIC and the NeurIPS city forum). Event and communication arrangements are conceptual suggestions; specific execution requires joint agreement by government, operating entities, and the community.
